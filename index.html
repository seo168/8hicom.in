01

Why the brand appears in several forms

People remove spaces, add a dot or join “com” to the name when typing from memory. Those variants can express the same intent, but search wording does not prove that every matching domain is official. Confirm the complete hostname before following an application, account or payment instruction.

02

8HI.COM is a name and an address pattern

The dotted spelling can refer to the brand wording or to a web address. Treat them separately: read the browser address character by character, confirm HTTPS and use a link from this reference network. Do not assume that a logo or page title validates the hostname beneath it.

  • 8HI and 8 HI: short brand forms
  • 8HICOM and 8HI COM: joined search forms
  • 8HI.COM: dotted brand form

Do not create a second account for a spelling variant

Brand variations are navigation terms, not separate applications or account systems. When account access becomes available, use the registered identifier and official recovery path. Creating duplicate accounts in response to a different spelling can complicate verification, payment review and reward eligibility.
